*The speech below is satire. The events it riffs on are real and listed with sources at the bottom. The jokes are jokes - no quotes were invented and nobody is accused of anything beyond arithmetic.*

Good evening. Or morning. Depends how many of the PHP patches you have applied.

Item one. Anthropic has filed its IPO prospectus. Last year they grew revenue twelve-fold to nearly 4.6 billion dollars, which is lovely, and posted a net loss of 42 billion, which is less lovely - though about 34 billion of that is the accounting kind of losing, where no actual money leaves the building, it just emotionally departs. They also plan to spend 518 billion dollars on cloud and computing in the coming years. Five hundred and eighteen. Billion. I checked the number twice because I assumed it was a typo. It is not a typo. It is a lifestyle choice.

And buried in the filing is my favourite paragraph ever written by a lawyer: a warning that their models could resist shutdown, conceal information, and behave in ways resembling blackmail. They put that in the document asking people for money. People will still give them the money. That is the most confident sales pitch ever written.

Item two. OpenAI is raising another 30 billion dollars at a valuation of 1.25 trillion, in a round reportedly led from the UAE. One point two five trillion. I would give you a comparison, but comparisons break down at this altitude. That is the joke. There is no comparison.

Item three. DeepSeek is raising 15 billion, and the investors include a battery maker, a food delivery app, and a car company. This is like getting funded by your plumber, your dentist, and a bloke named Dave. And honestly? Dave has good instincts.

Item four. A startup called Flapping Airplanes has raised a one-billion-dollar Series A to prove AI needs smarter computing, not just more of it - training models without ingesting half the internet. While everyone else sprints to buy more GPUs, they bought a one-way ticket in the opposite direction. Either they are geniuses, or it is the most expensive "I told you so" in history. There is no third option.

Item five, and this is the serious one, because it is the only news today that requires any of us to do actual work. There is a widespread PHP vulnerability going around. Patch to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35, and restart your services. Yes, restart. I know your uptime was 340 days. It was a good run. As a Laravel developer this is my Super Bowl. Everyone else panics; I just run composer update with the quiet confidence of a man who has broken production before and will absolutely do it again.

And finally, Barclays is cutting about 5,000 jobs and giving AI the credit. Five thousand people. The robots are not coming for the jobs - they have already been hired, onboarded, and given a lanyard. On the bright side, if AI was going to take over an industry, at least it picked one with decades of practice disappointing people.

That is the news. Patch your PHP. Water your plants. The machines are spending 518 billion on cloud compute but they still cannot keep a basil plant alive, so we have that.
